### Quantitative Experimental Results (Summary)

The 8-variant simulation demonstrated three critical physiological behaviors:
1. **SWS Autonomic Elevation**: Measuring RMSSD strictly during Slow Wave Sleep (SWS) yields **$37.72\text{ ms}$**, compared to **$29.70\text{ ms}$** across all quiet sleep ($+27.0\%$ higher), confirming why WHOOP values exceed raw actigraphy trackers.
2. **Nap Isolation Bug in STRAP**: STRAP currently weights daytime naps into `nightlyLnRMSSD` (`CaptureAnalysis.swift:215-226`), raising nocturnal HRV from $29.70\text{ ms}$ to $30.76\text{ ms}$ ($+3.6\%$), violating line 212's design comment. WHOOP isolates primary sleep for recovery and uses naps strictly as sleep debt credit.
3. **28-Day Baseline Resilience**: Under a 7-day baseline, a single acute disruption (alcohol/fever) inflates baseline variance by $+130\%$ ($\sigma_{\ln\text{HRV}}$ explodes from $0.108$ to $0.249$), causing premature, false Green rebounds ($67.5\%\text{--}69.4\%$). A 28-day baseline buffers the shock ($\sigma$ moves only to $0.155$), appropriately holding the recovery score in Yellow ($62.6\%\text{--}64.9\%$) until physiological equilibrium is restored.
